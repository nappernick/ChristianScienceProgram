# Rituals, Armor, and Spiritual Warfare - Image Generation Prompts

> "For we do not wrestle against flesh and blood, but against the rulers, against the authorities, against the cosmic powers over this present darkness, against the spiritual forces of evil in the heavenly places." - Ephesians 6:12

---

## Core Principle

The journey to find God is **simultaneously a battle against Satan**. As the Christian Space Program ventures deeper into the cosmos—further from Earth, further into the void—both the **intensity of spiritual warfare** and the **evolution of Christian practices** become more pronounced.

Practices that were comfortable on Earth become **strange and fierce** in deep space. The enemy is real. The armor is essential. The rituals are survival.

---

## SPIRITUAL WARFARE - The Missing Dimension

### The Reality of Satan in Space

**Theological Foundation:**
- Satan is the "prince of the power of the air" (Ephesians 2:2)
- Deep space = the ultimate "air" / cosmic void
- As crew approaches God's location, they approach Satan's domain
- Spiritual attacks intensify with distance from Earth
- Demonic manifestations possible (or are they?)

**Visual Questions:**
- Is Satan an actual being encountered in space?
- Are there demonic aliens/entities?
- Is spiritual warfare primarily internal (temptation, despair)?
- Do crew members see visions/hallucinations?
- How does evil manifest in the void?

## Notes for Image Generation

### Tone Balance

- **Not grimdark** - There is real evil but also real hope
- **Not horror** - Threatening but grounded in faith
- **Not fantasy** - Spiritual warfare is real but mysterious
- **Not comfortable** - This is battle, not peaceful devotion

### Realism Requirements

- Spiritual warfare manifests through:
  - Psychological strain (isolation, fear)
  - Equipment failures (coincidental or demonic?)
  - Visions and dreams (real or delusion?)
  - Oppressive atmospheres and presences
  - Internal temptations (despair, abandonment, apostasy)

- Physical manifestations:
  - Shadows that seem too present
  - Voices heard by multiple crew
  - Unexplained technical failures
  - Dreams that match across crew
  - Sense of being watched in the void

### Visual Intensity Progression

**Near Earth:**
- Simple faith practices
- Clean aesthetics
- Optimistic devotion
- Standard sacraments

**Mid-Journey:**
- Increased prayer frequency
- More icons and crosses
- Protection rituals added
- Spiritual vigilance grows

**Deep Space:**
- Fierce intense practices
- Walls covered in protective symbols
- Daily exorcisms normalized
- Sleep requires spiritual defense
- Everything is spiritual warfare
- Practices evolve beyond Earth recognition
- Desperate clinging to Christ
