# Image Generation Prompts - Christian Space Program

> Practical prompts for AI image generation to visualize the Christian Space Program

---

## Style Guidelines

### Overall Aesthetic
- **Realistic, not cartoonish** - This is inspiring real future
- **Reverent beauty** - Sacred art meets hard sci-fi
- **Human scale** - Even cosmic scenes ground in human experience
- **Light and shadow** - Chiaroscuro like Caravaggio
- **Detail and craftsmanship** - Nothing cheap or rushed

### Color Palettes

**Sacred Spaces:**
- Gold, white, deep blue
- Warm candlelight tones
- Stained glass color refractions
- Heavenly light

**Ship Exteriors:**
- Metallic grays and silvers
- Copper/bronze accents
- Stars as white/blue points
- Deep space black

**Alien Worlds:**
- Varies by planet (see Locations docs)
- Always show wonder and danger
- Spiritual weight in atmosphere

## Composition Principles

### For Inspiring Christians

1. **Show humans** - This is about people, not just tech
2. **Include prayer/worship** - Demonstrate integration of faith
3. **Realistic but hopeful** - Achievable future, not fantasy
4. **Beauty matters** - Offerings to God should be beautiful
5. **Sacred and practical** - Never separate the two
6. **Emotional resonance** - Move the viewer
7. **Theological depth** - Every image should raise questions

### Technical Specifications

**For all images:**
- Resolution: 8K or highest available
- Style: Photorealistic
- Lighting: Cinematic, considered
- Composition: Rule of thirds, golden ratio
- Detail level: High
- Color grading: Slightly desaturated for realism, warm tones for sacred

### Negative Prompts (What to Avoid)

```
Avoid: cartoon, anime, low quality, blurry, unrealistic, garish colors, superficial religious symbols, crosses slapped on things, generic sci-fi tropes, militaristic imagery, weapons-focused, dark/grimdark aesthetic, horror elements
```
