# Visual Style Reference - Christian Space Program

> **Mission**: Inspire Christians with realistic, beautiful vision of space exploration grounded in the Cross

---

## Core Visual Principles

### 1. REALISM FIRST
- **Not fantasy** - This should look achievable
- Photorealistic rendering
- Grounded in actual physics and engineering
- Materials that exist or could exist
- Lighting that follows physical laws

**Why:** Christians need to see this as **real possibility**, not escapist fiction

### 2. SACRED BEAUTY
- Beauty matters - offerings to God should be beautiful
- Reverent composition
- Considered lighting (chiaroscuro tradition)
- Visual theology - every image teaches truth
- Nothing cheap or hasty

**Why:** We serve the Author of beauty; our work should reflect His

### 3. THE CROSS IS CENTRAL
- Cross-shaped ships **always obvious**
- Cross imagery throughout
- Never hidden, never subtle
- Structural, not decorative

**Why:** "We preach Christ crucified" - 1 Corinthians 1:23

## Reference Traditions

### Sacred Art Traditions to Study

**Caravaggio & Chiaroscuro:**
- Dramatic lighting
- Deep shadows, brilliant highlights
- Spiritual weight in darkness/light contrast
- Human figures lit like divine revelation

**Gothic Cathedral Architecture:**
- Vertical emphasis (reaching toward God)
- Sacred geometry everywhere
- Light through colored glass
- Engineering as theology

**Byzantine Icons:**
- Gold backgrounds (divine light)
- Frontal poses (presence, not action)
- Symbolic color use
- Reverent, timeless quality

**Renaissance Composition:**
- Golden ratio in framing
- Triangular stability
- Human figures central
- Perspective drawing eye to focal point

### Science Fiction References (Use Cautiously)

**2001: A Space Odyssey:**
- Realistic spacecraft design
- Silence and scale of space
- Technology without romanticizing
- **AVOID:** mystical/pantheistic elements

**The Martian:**
- Grounded problem-solving
- Realistic tech
- Human perseverance
- **AVOID:** individualistic survivalism

**Interstellar:**
- Emotional weight of long journeys
- Love across vast distances
- Wonder at cosmic scale
- **AVOID:** relativism, humans-as-saviors

**AVOID ENTIRELY:**
- Star Wars (fantasy, not sci-fi)
- Star Trek (utopian humanism)
- Warhammer 40K (grimdark, militaristic)
- Marvel cosmic (superhero power-fantasy)

## Lighting Principles

### Three-Point Lighting with Theological Meaning

**Key Light (Father):**
- Primary illumination
- Often from above (heaven)
- Can be harsh or gentle based on scene
- Reveals form and truth

**Fill Light (Son):**
- Softens shadows (grace)
- Makes visible what was hidden
- Often warmer than key
- Incarnational - entering darkness

**Back Light (Spirit):**
- Rim lighting separating from background
- Ethereal quality
- Unexpected source
- Transformative glow

### Specific Lighting Scenarios

**Chapel/Worship Scenes:**
- Warm candlelight primary
- Starlight through windows (cool)
- Soft, reverent, peaceful
- Chiaroscuro (dramatic light/dark)

**Ship Exteriors in Space:**
- Single harsh light source (star)
- Deep blacks in shadow
- No atmospheric diffusion
- Stars as points, not glow
- Fusion drives self-illuminating

**Alien Worlds:**
- Alien sun color affects everything
- Atmospheric effects realistic
- Lighting conveys spiritual atmosphere
- Wonder through light quality

**Intimate Character Moments:**
- Soft, close lighting
- Face illumination primary
- Eyes visible and expressive
- Emotional warmth through light

## Composition Rules

### Rule of Thirds
- Horizon lines on third divisions
- Subject eyes on upper third line
- Cross intersection at power points
- **Exception:** Sacred subjects may be centered (icons)

### Golden Ratio (Phi = 1.618)
- Use throughout for divine proportion
- Ship dimensions follow golden ratio
- Frame divisions use phi
- Natural to eye, reflects creation order

### Leading Lines
- Guide eye to focal point
- Ship arms lead to hub/cross
- Architectural elements point to altar
- Paths lead to destination/God

### Sacred Symmetry
- Bilateral symmetry for reverent scenes
- Central altars, central crosses
- Face-forward poses for holiness
- Breaking symmetry shows movement/action

## Scale and Perspective

### Showing Vastness of Space
- Ships small against cosmic backdrops
- Humans tiny in vast ships
- Planets/stars showing true scale
- Humility in our smallness

### Showing Intimacy of Faith
- Close on praying faces
- Hands holding Bibles, touching controls
- Personal moments in vast void
- Individual matters infinitely

### Multi-Scale Compositions
- Wide establishing shot → medium → close-up
- Cosmic scale AND human detail
- "As above, so below"
- Fractal theology (pattern repeats at all scales)

## Human Figures

### Faces
- Realistic, varied ethnicities
- Expressive eyes (window to soul)
- Aged faces (wisdom, experience)
- Young faces (hope, future)
- Peaceful, not airbrushed
- Real emotions, not stock expressions

### Postures
- Prayer: hands folded, heads bowed, kneeling
- Work: engaged, purposeful, reverent
- Worship: lifting hands, faces upward
- Community: facing each other, touching
- Contemplation: still, quiet, attentive

### Clothing
- Simple robes or practical jumpsuits
- Crosses visible on all garments
- Nothing ostentatious
- Functional and beautiful
- Clerical garments for clergy (distinct)

### Ages
- Multi-generational always
- Elderly (wisdom, completion)
- Adults (strength, service)
- Youth (energy, questions)
- Children (hope, future)
- Babies (new life, promise)

## Technology Design Language

### Ships and Stations
- Cruciform primary shape
- Sacred geometry in all subsystems
- Golden ratio proportions
- Rose window patterns
- Cathedral architectural elements
- Visible crosses everywhere
- Industrial but reverent
- Worn and lived-in, not pristine

### Interiors
- Gothic arches and vaulting
- Clear sight lines to windows (stars)
- Integrated worship spaces
- Plants and life throughout
- Warm lighting mixed with starlight
- Books and analog alongside digital
- Human-scaled, not overwhelming

### Technology Aesthetics
- **NOT:** Sleek Apple minimalism
- **NOT:** Cyberpunk grungy chaos
- **YES:** Medieval craftsmanship meets engineering
- **YES:** Visible mechanisms (honesty)
- **YES:** Patina and use-wear (real life)
- **YES:** Beauty in function

## What to AVOID

### Visual Tropes to Reject

❌ **Dark, grimdark aesthetic** - This is hopeful
❌ **Militaristic imagery** - We're pilgrims, not soldiers
❌ **Sleek minimalism** - Life is rich and textured
❌ **Sexy/sensual imagery** - Keep pure and reverent
❌ **Individual hero focus** - Community matters more
❌ **Pristine perfection** - Real life has wear and beauty
❌ **Horror elements** - We face darkness with light
❌ **Triumphal conquest** - Humility, not pride
❌ **Generic crosses** - Make them obvious and structural
❌ **Alien monsters** - They're persons with souls

## Quality Standards

### Technical Minimums
- **Resolution:** 4K minimum, 8K preferred
- **Detail:** High, reward close examination
- **Focus:** Sharp where it matters, depth where needed
- **Noise:** Clean, not grainy (unless artistic choice)
- **Color:** Accurate, calibrated, meaningful

### Artistic Minimums
- **Composition:** Intentional, leads eye
- **Lighting:** Motivated, meaningful, beautiful
- **Emotion:** Present, authentic, moves viewer
- **Theology:** Accurate, deep, teaching
- **Beauty:** Always, as offering to God
