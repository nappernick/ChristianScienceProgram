## Holes this table shows

- Nothing between 2085 and departure has a date. Miriam is 56 at the full translation, so the tour, the golden age, the war and the ships all fall in her last decades or after her.
- Year 0 has no date, so nothing on the ship can be tied to an Earth year.
- None of the seven disciples has a name, a birth year or a folder.
