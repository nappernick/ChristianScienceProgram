# Hess: Economics

*Proposed.*

- **Income:** paid per dive day, plus a small naval pension.
- **Assets:** a nine-meter sloop in Bremerhaven. Nothing else of value.
- **Obligations:** his daughter's university costs, which he pays in full and has never mentioned to her.
- **Liability:** the vehicle's manipulator arm costs roughly a year of his pay. Jettisoning it triggers an inquiry by the operator and, usually, the end of that pilot's contract with them.
- **Certification:** a pilot's medical is renewed yearly. The operator retires pilots at fifty-six.

## What this does to his choices

- **He was ready to drop the arm anyway.** His rule would have cost him the contract and perhaps the last two seasons of his career. He lifted the guard without hesitating. That is who he is, shown through money.
- Not dropping it saved him an inquiry. He knows that. He does not know whether it shaded his decision to wait, and it bothers him.
- He has two seasons left and a daughter to finish paying for. He will not go looking for trouble with the sponsors. He will also not lie for them.
