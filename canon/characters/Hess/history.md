# Hess: History

*Hess is from chapter two. Everything here beyond what that chapter shows is a proposal.*

**Fixed by the chapter**

- Pilot of the *Wren*. Does not narrate, does not point things out.
- Operates the vehicle as an extension of his body.
- Leaves the scientists to their work. "Twenty minutes" is more than he usually offers.

**Proposed**

- **Born** 2021, Bremerhaven. 54 at the dive. First name Jonas, as a placeholder.
- **His mother** cleaned churches. As a boy he sat in empty ones for hours while she worked. He still goes into churches in every port, sits at the back, and leaves. It is how he knows what is written over Wren's tomb.
- **Navy,** 2040 to 2052, as a submarine rescue technician.
- **The rescue,** 2052. A research submersible fouled in the rigging of a wreck at six hundred meters. Its pilot worked the problem for nine hours instead of cutting free and losing the vehicle. The rescue vehicle, with Hess aboard, reached them at hour thirty-one. Two of three were alive.
- **The rule** comes from that. You decide beforehand. If it fouls, you drop it.
- **Civilian pilot** since 2053. Twenty-two years, more than four thousand hours on the bottom.
- **Divorced** 2060. One daughter, Lena, born 2054, at university in Hamburg.
