# Hess: Philosophy

*Proposed.*

- **Decide before, not during.** A person under pressure is a worse version of the person who made the plan. Trust the earlier one.
- **A vehicle is safe while it can leave.** Everything else is detail.
- **Do not narrate.** The people in the cabin have their own work. Talking about yours takes from theirs.
- **The sea is not interested.** He does not think of it as hostile or beautiful. It is where the work is.
- **A wreck is somebody's.** He looks at anything on the bottom and asks what the people were doing when it went wrong. He cannot stop doing this.
- **On religion:** not disclosed, to anyone, including the reader. He sits in churches. He has never said why. He does not use the word "God" as an oath or as anything else.

## Where this collides with Miriam

He believes decisions belong to the person who made them calmly, in advance. She overturned one of his in nine seconds by sounding sure. She was right. He does not know what to do with a case where breaking the rule was correct, and he will keep turning it over.
