# Hess: Psychology

*Proposed.*

- **Core wound:** hour thirty-one. He was on the vehicle that arrived too late for one of three, and he has spent twenty-three years as the kind of pilot who would not have needed rescuing.
- **What he built on it:** rules made in advance, stated aloud, and followed.
- **Temperament:** still. Patience in him is not restraint. It is how he is.
- **A childhood stammer,** trained out by saying less. Brevity began as a workaround.
- **Under stress:** he goes procedural and speaks for the recorder.
- **Humor:** dry, literal, and rare enough to be missed.
- **Toward the scientists:** respect, shown by leaving them alone.
- **What he notices:** hands, postures, how people sit when they are frightened.
- **What he cannot do:** explain himself.
- **Blind spot:** he thinks that because he does not offer opinions, he has not taken a side.
