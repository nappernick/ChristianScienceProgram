# Hess: Body

*Proposed, except where marked.*

- **Age 54.** Broad, not tall. Built to fit a sphere.
- **Hands:** large and exact. He flies with two fingers *(from the drafts)*.
- **Knees:** bad. Getting in and out of the hatch is the hardest part of his day.
- **Ears:** tinnitus, a constant high note, from years beside compressors. Silence is never quite silent for him.
- **Eyes:** good in the dark, slow in bright light.
- **Stillness:** he can sit without shifting for eight hours.
- **Before a dive:** he eats almost nothing and drinks no coffee.
- **Implant:** none. The operator's certification bars neural interfaces for pilots. The vehicle is flown by hands and eyes only.

## What this does to his choices

- **He felt the contact.** With no implant, everything the vehicle tells him comes through his fingers. "It gave" is a statement about what his hands registered, and it is better evidence than the number on the tablet.
- No implant also means no private record. What he knows is in his head and nowhere else.
- His knees and his medical are why this is near the end. He is flying the last dives of his life, and one of them is this one.
