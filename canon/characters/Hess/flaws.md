# Hess: Flaws

*Proposed.*

## The flaw

He yields to certainty in other people, and he does not speak unless asked.

## Why he is flawed this way

The 2052 rescue taught him to distrust his own judgment in the moment, which is why he makes rules. It left a gap: he has no defense against someone else's judgment in the moment, if they sound calm. And twelve years in a service where the technician does not volunteer opinions to the officer made silence his default.

## How it shows

- He states his condition in advance, then gives it up when Miriam says "wait."
- He answers what he is asked and stops.
- He knows things about the dive that nobody else does, and it has not occurred to him to tell them.

## How it moves the story

1. **He obeys her once, with no reason to.** It is the first time her certainty moves a person who owes her nothing. She notices.
2. **He is the only witness to the contact.** He felt it through the controls. It gave. Everyone else saw a number.
3. **His silence makes room for the legend.** The liturgy calls him the Silent One and praises him for it. The actual man could correct most of the Vigil and never has.
4. **He tells the truth when asked directly.** So the story needs someone to ask. Whoever does, and when, is a hinge.
5. **Every boy named Hess on the ship is named for a man the church admires for not talking.** His flaw has been made a virtue and handed to children.
