# Hess: Choices

*Proposed.*

| Situation | What he does | Because |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A risky task | States his condition first, aloud | Decide before, not during |
| The condition is met | Acts on it at once | The earlier self is the trustworthy one |
| Someone calm tells him to wait | Waits | He has no defense against another person's certainty |
| Something goes wrong | Speaks for the recorder | Procedure steadies him |
| The scientists are working | Says nothing, does the useful thing unasked | Respect |
| Told to keep quiet by the sponsors | Keeps quiet | It is his habit anyway |
| Asked a direct question | Answers it truthfully, in under ten words | He does not lie |
| Asked to say something untrue | Declines, without a speech | He does not lie |
| Nobody asks | Nothing is said | His flaw |

## The line he will not cross

He will not sign or say a false account of a dive. He has been in the room after a dive went wrong, and he knows what the record is for.
