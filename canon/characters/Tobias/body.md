# Tobias Adeyemi: Body

- **Age 24.** Strong, quick, sure-footed on a moving deck.
- **Voice:** he sings while he works, hymns mostly, without knowing he is doing it.
- **Scar:** left forearm, from a winch cable in his first season.
- **Sleep:** anywhere, instantly.
- **Hands:** he knows the *Wren* by touch. He can tell which seal is tired by how a hatch dogs down.
- **Implant:** a cheap consumer model, always on. It records voice notes and syncs them whenever the ship has a link, which is every night.

## What this does to his choices

- He is the one who repairs the scored joint on the wand. He has his hands on the physical evidence before anyone tells him what it touched.
- **The implant is how the news leaves the ship.** He does not send a message so much as fail to stop one. He talks to his mother at the end of the day, as he always does, and the sync does the rest.
- His body is at home on the ship in a way nobody else's is. He belongs there, and that is why putting him ashore is a real punishment.
