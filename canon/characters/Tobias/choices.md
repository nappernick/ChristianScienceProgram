# Tobias Adeyemi: Choices

| Situation | What he does | Because |
| --- | --- | --- |
| He learns something good | Tells the people he loves | Testimony is a duty |
| A fact is cautious and partial | Rounds it up to the warm version | He needs it to be true |
| Someone is certain | Assumes they share his faith | Certainty looks like belief to him |
| Miriam is cold to him | Prays for her and tries again | It would not occur to him that he is the problem |
| Told to keep quiet | Agrees sincerely, then forgets | Secrets do not stay in him |
| Accused of the leak | Admits it immediately, puzzled | He has done nothing he is ashamed of |
| Offered a way to blame someone else | Refuses | He does not lie |
| Put ashore | Tells everyone what happened to him | Which is how the story spreads further |

## The line he will not cross

He will not deny what he saw or said. That is what makes him useless to Carrow and, later, valuable to Miriam.
