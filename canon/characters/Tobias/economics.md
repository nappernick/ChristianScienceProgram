# Tobias Adeyemi: Economics

- **Pay:** a deck technician's wage on a rolling contract, renewed voyage by voyage. The lowest-paid skilled person aboard.
- **Where it goes:** a third to his mother, a tenth to the church, the rest saved toward a marine engineer's ticket.
- **Debt:** none. He came up through an apprenticeship.
- **Security:** none. Contract crew can be put ashore at the next port without a reason.
- **What he signed:** a confidentiality agreement, on his first day, without reading it.

## What this does to his choices

- **He is the cheapest person to punish.** When the leak has to be somebody's fault, he costs nothing to lose and has no one to speak for him.
- A dismissal for breach would follow him. He would not work on a survey ship again.
- He does not know any of this is at stake when he sends the message. He thinks he is telling his church about a good day.
- Forty people at home pray for him by name. He owes them news, in a way that feels more binding to him than a form he signed.
