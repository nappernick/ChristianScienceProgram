# Tobias Adeyemi: Psychology

- **Position in the family:** youngest of three sons, the only one not in ministry.
- **What that left him with:** a mild, permanent wish to bring his father something.
- **Temperament:** open. He likes people and assumes they like him, and is usually right.
- **Joy:** real, not a performance. He has not yet lost anyone or been badly wrong about anything.
- **Irony:** none. He says what he means and takes others at their word.
- **Under stress:** he talks more, and prays aloud without noticing.
- **When accused:** he confesses at once. It would not occur to him to deny.
- **What he admires:** people who are sure. He reads certainty as faith.
- **What he cannot do:** keep good news.
- **Blind spot:** he has never met a true thing that needed to wait, so he does not believe there are any.
