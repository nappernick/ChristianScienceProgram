## What is running out

Nothing, as far as he knows. He is the one person aboard who believes he has all the time in the world. Everyone else is working against a clock, and he is singing.

## Where he is in his life

At the beginning. He has a trade he loves, a family that prays for him, and no losses. The find is the best thing that has ever happened to him, and it will cost him the trade.
