# Tobias Adeyemi: History

*Deck technician on the 2075 survey. New in this pass. Added to fix a gap in the cast: see `_ensemble.md`.*

- **Born** 2051, Lagos. Raised in Peckham, south London, from the age of six. 24 at the time of the dive.
- **Family.** His father pastors a Pentecostal congregation of about four hundred. His two older brothers went into ministry. Tobias was the one who liked engines.
- **Training.** Marine engineering apprenticeship, then launch-and-recovery work on survey ships. He is good at it. He services the *Wren* between dives and knows her better than anyone but Hess.
- **Aboard.** He is the person everyone likes. He runs the card game, knows the cook's children's names, and sends his mother a voice message every night.
- **His church group.** Forty people at home who pray for him by name while he is at sea, and to whom he sends a longer message every Sunday about what he has seen.
- **At the dive.** He is one of the two deck crew who take the lines at recovery and are then sent below. He has already seen Hess's face coming out of the hatch.
- **The scored arm.** He is the one who has to repair the bright crescent on the wand's second joint. He asks Hess what it hit. Hess does not say.

## Proposed arc

- First believer to learn what was found.
- First person to tell anyone outside the ship.
- First follower. The mass movement in your Bible has to start with one person who is simply glad, and he is that person.
