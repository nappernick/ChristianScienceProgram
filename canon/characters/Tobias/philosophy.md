# Tobias Adeyemi: Philosophy

- **God is near and ordinary.** He says "God" the way other people say "the weather." It is not a claim he is making. It is the air he grew up in.
- **Joy is evidence.** If a thing makes people glad and whole, that counts for it.
- **Testimony is a duty.** If you have seen something, you tell it. Keeping it to yourself is a kind of theft from the people who needed to hear.
- **Work is worship.** He would never put it that way. But he services a submersible as if somebody were going to inspect it who mattered.
- **He does not need proof.** He already believed. For him the find is not a revelation. It is a confirmation, like getting a letter from someone you knew was alive.

## Where this collides with Miriam

Your Bible gives the line exactly: *you believe because you chose to. I believe because I was forced to. You find this comforting. I find this annihilating.* Tobias is the first person she thinks that about. He is kind, and he is thrilled for her, and he wants to pray with her, and she cannot bear to be in the room.

## Where this collides with everyone else

- **Dunmore** thinks he is a fool and says so. Tobias is not hurt, which Dunmore finds intolerable.
- **Carrow** thinks he is a leak. Carrow is right.
- **Hess** likes him and tells him nothing.
