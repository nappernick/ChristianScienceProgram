# Wren: Body

- **Age 15.** Tall and long-limbed, the build of someone raised at part weight.
- **Where she grew up:** the port arm, a third of the way out, where a body weighs about a third of what it does at the rim.
- **In the hub** she is completely at home. She has moved in zero gravity since before she could walk.
- **At the rim** she tires quickly and her knees ache. Ship children race out along the arm to feel themselves get heavy, and are glad to come back.
- **Eyes:** adapted to low light. The farthest thing she has ever looked at inside the ship is the far end of an arm. The stars do not count; nobody can judge how far away they are.
- **Hands:** the beginnings of a welder's calluses.
- **Things her body has never met:** wind, rain, cold, a horizon, an animal.

## What this does to her choices

- **The Vigil's dark does not frighten her.** She sees better in it than the old people do, and she can tell by sound who is near. It is why the hub is where she feels least watched.
- "Eleven hundred meters" means nothing to her muscles. She has no bodily sense of depth, weight of water, or cold, so the Descent is words.
- If she is ever shown a real recording of the dive, the breathing and the cold in the voices will be the first thing she understands that the liturgy never gave her.
