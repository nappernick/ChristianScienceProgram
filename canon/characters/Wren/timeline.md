## What is running out

- **A year.** At the end of it she stands alone in the dark and speaks for three thousand people.
- **The time in which she can still be a girl who says nothing.** A Reader is watched.

## Where she is in her life

At the edge of being given a place. Until now nobody expected anything of her but competence. From here on, what she thinks is somebody's business.
