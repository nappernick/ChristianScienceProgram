# Wren: Choices

| Situation | What she does | Because |
| --- | --- | --- |
| She wants to know something | Tests it quietly and watches | Asking closes the subject |
| Asked a direct question | Gives the expected answer, clearly | Shame at being noticed |
| Asked the same question by someone who has told her a truth first | Answers honestly | Perpetua found the one key |
| Offered the Reader's place | Accepts | Her father comes off the hull |
| Given access to something hidden | Looks, carefully, and tells nobody | Pride in noticing, and the habit of silence |
| Finds something that contradicts the liturgy | Sits on it | She can hold two readings at once |
| Her silence would let someone else be harmed | Speaks | This is the edge of her flaw, and the story is walking her toward it |

## The line she will not cross

She will omit. She will not state a thing she believes to be false as her own belief. Saying the replies is not a lie to her, because everyone says them. Saying "I am sure" would be.
