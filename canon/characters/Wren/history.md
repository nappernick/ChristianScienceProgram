# Wren: History

*Fifteen years old in Year 304 of the Pilgrimage. Point of view for the ship sections.*

- **Born** Year 289, port arm.
- **Family.** Her father is a hull welder in the Wrights, the guild that maintains the ship's structure. Wrights have named daughters Wren for two centuries, because the first Wren was a builder of churches and they are builders of one (see `canon/lore/naming_the_wrens.md`). Her mother works the wheat decks. Her brother Hess is six.
- **Schooling.** Sister Perpetua taught her to read. She was quick at recitation and was marked early as a possible Reader.
- **The question at five.** She asked why the Pause is as long as it is and was told it was the length of the Pause.
- **The question at twelve.** She asked again, a different catechist, and was told that waiting is a kind of trust. She noticed this was a better answer and still not one.
- **The glass.** At fifteen she stood in front of the likeness of the Notebook and thought the handwriting looked careful. She has told no one.
- **This year's Vigil.** She stayed silent at one reply, as an experiment. Sister Perpetua heard it.
- **What has just happened.** Her name has gone forward to be next year's Reader.

## What the role will give her

Readers are prepared for a year. Part of the preparation is access that ordinary crew never get, including presence at the Opening of the reliquary if one falls in their year. That is the door this character exists to walk through.
