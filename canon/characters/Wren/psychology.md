# Wren: Psychology

- **First child,** with a brother born when she was nine. She learned to look after someone before she learned to argue with anyone.
- **Temperament:** watchful. She would sooner see how a thing works than be told.
- **What early questions taught her:** asking aloud closes a subject. Watching keeps it open.
- **Shame:** being noticed. She does not mind being wrong. She minds being seen to wonder.
- **Pride:** noticing. She believes, privately, that she sees what others miss.
- **Under stress:** she says the expected thing very clearly and goes still.
- **Toward authority:** obedient on the surface and unconvinced underneath, without resentment. She does not think the catechists are lying. She thinks they have stopped looking.
- **What she wants:** somewhere to stand while not knowing.
- **What she cannot do yet:** say a true thing to a person who has not asked for it.
- **Blind spot:** she takes her own silence for honesty. It is closer to safety.
