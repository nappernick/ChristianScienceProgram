# Dr. Kamala Iyer: Philosophy

- **The number outranks the story.** A measurement does not care who made it or what it implies. If the implication is absurd, check the measurement again, and then accept the implication.
- **Procedure is protection.** A truth is most at risk from the people who want it. "Everyone in the room" is how you stop one person's wanting from bending the result.
- **Authority belongs to competence.** A decision outside your field is not yours to make. You hand it to whoever's field it is, and then you back them. This is a principle for her, and also a relief.
- **Patience is a method.** She waited eight years once. She believes most errors are errors of hurry.
- **On religion:** lapsed and incurious. Temple at weddings. She regards the God question as outside her field, which for her settles whether she needs a view.
- **What she would say about the find:** "I dated a rock. I stand by the rock."

## Where this collides with Miriam

Iyer thinks a truth is safe once it has been through the room. Miriam is about to hold a truth that cannot be taken through any room, because nobody else can read it. Iyer has no category for that.
