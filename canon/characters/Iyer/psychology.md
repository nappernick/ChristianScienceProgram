# Dr. Kamala Iyer: Psychology

- **Core wound:** eight years of being right and alone. She does not fear being wrong. She fears being right with nobody beside her.
- **What she built on it:** trust in the room over trust in herself. Consensus feels like safety.
- **Shame trigger:** being called sloppy. "Contamination" is the one word that reaches her.
- **Under stress:** she reruns the work. Literally. At two in the morning she is back at the mass spectrometer.
- **Conflict style:** concede a small point first, then hold the large one without moving.
- **Relation to authority:** she wants to be the authority inside her field and nowhere else. Outside it she hands decisions away quickly and feels relief.
- **What comforts her:** instrument time, alone, with a clean baseline.
- **What she cannot do:** act on a conviction that has not yet been agreed with.
- **Blind spot:** she thinks handing a decision to the right expert discharges her responsibility for it.
