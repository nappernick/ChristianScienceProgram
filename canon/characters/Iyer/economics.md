# Dr. Kamala Iyer: Economics

- **Income:** a chief scientist's day rate, paid by the consortium, for roughly five months at sea a year. Good money, no security.
- **Base:** a rented flat in Southampton, near the oceanography centre she no longer works for.
- **Dependents:** her mother in Chennai, ninety, in private care that costs more each year.
- **Savings:** thin. Leaving the university at forty-six cost her most of a pension.
- **What she does not own:** her instruments, her samples, or her results. The survey contract gives the sponsors every finding, with an eighteen-month embargo before she may publish.
- **What a wrong date would cost:** her next contract. A chief scientist who announces an impossible number and is shown to have dirty samples is not hired again.
- **What a right date is worth:** nothing she can spend, until the embargo lifts.

## What this does to her choices

- She cannot afford to be reckless and cannot afford to be silent. Procedure is the only position that is safe either way.
- She will comply with the sponsors' man and resent it, because the contract is clear and her mother's fees are due.
- "Everyone in the room" also means everyone shares the blame.
