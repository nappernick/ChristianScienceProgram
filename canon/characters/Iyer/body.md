# Dr. Kamala Iyer: Body

- **Age 58.** Small, upright, quick-moving on land and careful on a wet deck.
- **Hands:** arthritic from thirty years of fine sample work. Cold makes them worse. She keeps them in her sleeves on deck.
- **Ears:** surgery on the left at forty for a ruptured eardrum. She is not cleared to dive and never will be.
- **Eyes:** reading glasses on a cord, pushed up into her hair when she argues.
- **Sleep:** poor at sea. She is often the only person awake at three, which is when she reruns things.
- **Implant:** a basic one, used for instrument feeds. She can watch a spectrum build with her eyes closed.
- **Voice:** low and exact. On the acoustic link it flattens to gray like everyone's, but her pauses are recognizable.

## What this does to her choices

- She experiences the most important event of her career as a voice with a three-second hole in it. She never sees the thing in place. Everything she knows about the site, she was told.
- She is physically dependent on the people who go down, which reinforces handing them the call.
- The night hours are when she changes her mind, and nobody sees it happen.
