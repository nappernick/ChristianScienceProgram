# Dr. Kamala Iyer: Flaws

## The flaw

She needs to be vindicated, and she needs the vindication to arrive through proper procedure. She cannot act on being right. She can only wait to be agreed with.

## Why she is flawed this way

The eight years. Being right and alone was the worst period of her life, and what ended it was not her own certainty but the field finally coming round. She learned to trust the room more than herself. She also learned to hear every doubt as a replay of that mockery, which is why Dunmore gets under her skin.

## How it shows

- "Her" basalt. She is proprietary about a date in a way a careful scientist should not be.
- She will not make a call outside her field, even when she is the senior person present.
- Whoever confirms her number gains a hold on her that she does not notice.

## How it moves the story

1. **It hands Miriam her first authority.** "Your call" is a principled act and an abdication at once. The chief scientist steps back, and a linguist who has been aboard a month is now deciding what happens to the most important object ever found.
2. **It gives Miriam her first lever.** "You'll want pictures before you tell anybody what's inside *your* basalt." Miriam offers vindication and gets permission. It is the first time she moves a person by reading what they need.
3. **It makes Iyer the first brake.** Once the find is real, Iyer wants the room, the plan, the consensus. Miriam wants speed. Iyer becomes the first person Miriam has to go around, and the going-around is cleaner because Iyer handed over the authority herself.
4. **It gives her a tragic shape.** The woman who gave Miriam her start spends decades insisting she only vouched for a rock.
