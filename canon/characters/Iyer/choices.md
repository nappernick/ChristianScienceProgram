# Dr. Kamala Iyer: Choices

How the rest of her folder predicts what she does.

| Situation | What she does | Because |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A result nobody believes | Reruns it, then defends it without raising her voice | The eight years |
| A decision outside her field | Hands it to the specialist and backs them | Principle, and relief |
| Pressure from the sponsors | Complies on paper, insists on a proper copy in the safe | The contract, her mother's fees, and her faith in procedure |
| Someone vindicates her number | Becomes generous toward them, more than she notices | She has been waiting to be agreed with |

| Miriam wants speed | Asks for the plan and the room | Haste is where errors come from |

## The line she will not cross

She will never falsify or soften a measurement. Everything else is negotiable under enough pressure.
