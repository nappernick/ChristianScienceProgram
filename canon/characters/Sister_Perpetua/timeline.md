## What is running out

- **Her own time.** She wants one more Reader who holds, and a successor.
- **Anselm.** He is as old as she is.

## The Opening of 275

She was in the room. The reliquary was opened to the Descent page, the abbot looked, and it was closed. She has always said that is all that happened. Whether the abbot's hand moved toward the corner of the page before he closed it is something only the people in that room know, and she is one of two still living.

## Where she is in her life

Near the end, with her method apparently vindicated and one thing she has never said about a room she stood in thirty years ago.
