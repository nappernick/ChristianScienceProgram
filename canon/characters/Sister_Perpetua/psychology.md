# Sister Perpetua: Psychology

- **Core wound:** Anselm. She was taken in gently for her doubt, and a year later watched a friend be set apart for his. The difference between them was that he said it aloud.
- **What she built on it:** a vocation. She finds the quiet ones early and brings them inside before they can speak.
- **Self-image:** a rescuer. She has put forward eleven children and counts each as saved.
- **Guilt:** she survived by being absorbed, and has made a career of absorbing others. She does not look straight at that.
- **Warmth:** real. Children trust her because she is trustworthy in every way that does not threaten the ship.
- **Under stress:** practical. She gives an instruction about something small.
- **Her faith:** she is not sure, has not been sure since sixteen, and has decided that being sure was never what was asked of her.
- **What she fears most:** a child set apart on her watch.
- **What she cannot do:** let someone speak and wait to see what happens.
- **Blind spot:** she has never asked Anselm whether he regrets it.
