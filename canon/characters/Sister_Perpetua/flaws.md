# Sister Perpetua: Flaws

## The flaw

She manages doubt in other people by drawing it close and making it useful, and she calls this care. It is care. It is also control, and she has stopped being able to tell the two apart.

## Why she is flawed this way

Anselm. She watched what happened to a doubter who spoke, the year after she had been quietly absorbed for the same doubt. She concluded that the kind thing is to catch the bright ones early and give them a place inside. Every child she has put forward since is a child she believes she saved.

## How it shows

- She tells Wren the truth ("I have always thought so too") and uses the truth to bind her.
- She confides one thing, once, and then closes the subject. It is exactly the method your Bible describes Miriam using on her disciples: show the crack, never the abyss.
- She is gentle. Nobody raises a voice. That is what makes it hard to refuse.

## How it moves the story

1. **She is the gate.** Without her, Wren stays a quiet girl in the rings. With her, Wren is Reader-elect with a year of access ahead.
2. **She is ally and antagonist in one person.** She will protect Wren up to the moment Wren speaks. After that she is the one whose duty it is to act. The reader should like her and dread her.
3. **She explains the Hum.** Your Bible says the ships hum like someone trying not to hear something. Perpetua shows how: for generations the institution has taken its sharpest doubters and promoted them. The people leading the liturgy are the people least sure of it, and they lead it very well.
4. **She carries Miriam's method forward without knowing its source.** The control mechanism of the founding is alive in a schoolteacher three hundred years later. That is the legacy made visible at human scale.
