# Sister Perpetua: Body

- **Age 63,** which is old aboard. Three centuries of low weight and background radiation have shortened lives.
- **Bones:** brittle. Full weight at the rim hurts her. She lives near the hub and walks the arm slowly with a hand on the rail.
- **Ears:** exceptional. Forty years of listening to children recite in a dark hub. She can pick one missing voice out of a ring.
- **Eyes:** cataracts beginning. She can no longer read the likeness of the Notebook through its glass. She knows what it says and how it looks from memory.
- **Hands:** small and exact. She still corrects a letter by taking the child's hand and moving it.
- **Voice:** quiet enough that people lean in.

## What this does to her choices

- **She heard Wren's breathing change.** Nobody else could have. Her body is the instrument the practice runs on.
- Her failing eyes mean her certainty about the Notebook's "careful" handwriting is now a memory of a judgment. She cannot check it.
- She is running out of years in which she can walk the arm. Wren may be the last child she chooses, and she chooses accordingly.
