# Sister Perpetua: Philosophy

- **Certainty is something people do for each other.** No single person in the rings is sure. The sound of all of them saying the replies clearly is what lets the person beside them be. She thinks this is the most beautiful thing on the ship and she is not being ironic.
- **Belief is a load-bearing wall.** The ship cannot turn round and cannot stop. If enough people decide there is nothing ahead, people die. So doubt is not a sin to her. It is a weight, and weights have to be carried carefully.
- **What you may think and where you may say it are separate questions.** She thinks the Founder's handwriting looks careful. She has always thought so. She would never say it from the Reader's place.
- **The Founder was careful, and that is why she loves her.** Most of the ship loves a woman who was certain. Perpetua loves one who was not, and who held it together anyway. She believes she understands the Founder better than the hardliners do.
- **The Pause is the first lag.** She holds a minority view, passed down among the people who work the long-range receivers, that all prayer is a message sent across a delay of unknown length. You say your whole thing, you stop, you wait.

## What she cannot see

She has taken her own way of surviving and called it wisdom. It may be wisdom. She has never tested it against the alternative, because the alternative is in the compost chambers.
