# Sister Perpetua: History

*Catechist of the port arm. Sixty-three in Year 304.*

- **Born** Year 241.
- **The gap.** At sixteen, in Year 257, she stayed silent at the second reply of the Vigil. She had planned it for weeks. Nothing happened, and that frightened her more than punishment would have.
- **Reader.** She was made Reader the following year. This is the standing practice for bright children who are heard to waver (see `canon/lore/the_readers_cure.md`).
- **Anselm.** In her Reader year, a friend of hers, Anselm, a boy from the wheat decks, was Reader-elect for the year after. At his own Vigil he stopped in the Silence and said aloud that he did not think anyone was there. He was not punished in any way the ship would call punishment. He was reassigned to the compost chambers in the lower arm, where the dead are returned to the gardens, and he has not tethered in the rings since. He is still alive. She visits him at the turn of each year and they do not speak about it.
- **Vows.** She took vows at nineteen, the year after Anselm.
- **Forty-four years of letters.** She has taught reading to most of the port arm. She has put forward eleven names for Reader. Nine of the eleven were children she had heard go quiet.
- **This year.** She heard Wren's breath change at the fourth reply, and put her name forward the same night.
