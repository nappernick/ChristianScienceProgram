# Sister Perpetua: Choices

| Situation | What she does | Because |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A child goes quiet at a reply | Notes it, waits a year or two, then puts the name forward | Catch them before they speak |
| The child denies it | "No, you didn't." Without anger | She will not let a lie stand between them |
| The child asks a real question | Tells one true thing, once, and closes the subject | It binds, and it is all she can afford |
| A family presses for a nomination | Declines the gift, considers the child | She is invested, not for sale |
| Her chosen Reader wavers in the year | Draws closer. More time, more trust, more access | Absorption is the only tool she believes in |
| Her chosen Reader speaks aloud | Reports it herself, gently, the same night | Better from her than from anyone else. And Anselm |
| Asked what she saw at the Opening | Says the page was not turned | It is true as far as it goes |
| Offered the chance to turn the page herself | Refuses | She has decided what she can live with knowing |

## The line she will not cross

She will not lie to a child's face. She will leave things out, close subjects, and act against them afterward. She will not say a false thing to them.
