# Dr. Alistair Dunmore: Body

- **Age 61.** Tall, heavy, slow on ladders.
- **Hip:** replaced at fifty-eight. He cannot fold himself into a submersible and has never been below.
- **Voice:** large. It carries across a mess and through a bulkhead. On the acoustic link he is the small noise behind Iyer.
- **Drink:** two glasses at dinner, which is when he argues.
- **Implant:** none, on principle. He reads printouts and carries a pencil.
- **Hands:** a petrologist's. He picks up every rock sample and turns it over before he will discuss it.
- **Sleep:** little. He works at night, alone, in the dry lab.

## What this does to his choices

- Like Iyer, he never sees the site. Unlike her, he does not trust the people who did.
- He will not accept an image as evidence. He wants the object in his hand, and when it is in his hand he will be honest about what he feels.
- The printouts and the pencil mean his private checking leaves a paper trail. Someone could find it.
