# Dr. Alistair Dunmore: Economics

- **Income:** consulting fees as an expert assessor, plus a pension from a university that paid him well for thirty years. The survey work is pocket money.
- **Assets:** a house in Fife, paid off. No debts.
- **Dependents:** none. No children. His wife is dead.
- **What he needs from the sponsors:** nothing.

## What this does to his choices

- **He is the only person aboard who cannot be pressured through money.** Iyer has her mother's care. Natasha has a contract ending. Tobias can be sacked. Carrow has a bonus. Dunmore can say whatever he likes.
- That is why the dissent comes from him. It is not only temperament. He is the one who can afford it.
- It also makes him easy to dismiss. A man with nothing at stake is assumed to be arguing for sport.
- He cannot be bought. He can be isolated, and that is how he will be handled.
