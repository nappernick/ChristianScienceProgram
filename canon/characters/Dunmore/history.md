# Dr. Alistair Dunmore: History

*Petrologist on the 2075 survey. The skeptic at dinner. First name is a placeholder.*

- **Born** 2014, Aberdeen. 61 at the time of the dive.
- **Field:** igneous petrology. He reads how rock formed from its texture and chemistry.
- **The debunking.** At thirty-four he took apart a celebrated claim of fossil microbes in a meteorite. The claim's author was a senior, well-loved figure. Dunmore was correct on every point and delivered it in a way that ended the older man's career in public. He has never understood why people remember the manner and not the result.
- **The record.** Since then he has been the person journals send extraordinary claims to. By his own count he has been asked to assess forty-one and has found forty-one mundane explanations. He is proud of the number.
- **Why he is at sea.** Same collapse of research funding as Iyer. He takes survey contracts for the pay and treats the work as beneath him.
- **On this expedition.** He attributes Iyer's date to contamination from an older stratum, which is the most likely explanation and the one any competent petrologist would offer first.
- **At the dive.** He is in the comms shack, behind Iyer, saying "multipath."

## Proposed arc after chapter three

- He does not convert. When the object is undeniable he accepts the object and moves his skepticism one step back: the artifact is real, the reading of it is one woman's.
- He becomes the first articulate voice of the problem at the center of your Bible: nobody can check her deepest translation.
