## What is running out

- **His relevance.** Journals increasingly use models to referee. Nobody sends him the strange papers any more.
- **Opponents.** The people he enjoyed fighting have retired or died.

## Where he is in his life

A widower at sea for want of anywhere better, with a perfect record and no one left to show it to. The find is the forty-second claim. It is the first one that will not go away, and it arrives when he has the least to lose and the least to live for.
