# Dr. Alistair Dunmore: Psychology

- **Where he comes from:** a Church of Scotland manse. His father was a minister, gentle and patient. Dunmore lost his faith at fifteen and announced it at Sunday lunch, wanting a fight. His father said, "Well, you'll have thought about it," and passed the potatoes.
- **What that did:** he has been looking for the fight ever since. Every claim he demolishes is the argument his father would not have.
- **Core fear:** being taken in. Being the one who believed.
- **Humor:** a weapon first and a pleasure second.
- **Under stress:** louder, then silent.
- **Grief:** his wife died in 2071. He has not spoken about it to anyone aboard and takes sea contracts to be out of the house.
- **What he cannot do:** say "I was wrong" where anyone can hear.
- **What he does in private:** checks. At night he reruns other people's numbers. He is more honest alone than in company.
- **Blind spot:** he believes his manner is the price of rigor. It is separate from his rigor and costs him every argument that matters.
