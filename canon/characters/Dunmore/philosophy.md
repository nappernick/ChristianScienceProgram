# Dr. Alistair Dunmore: Philosophy

- **The dull explanation is nearly always the true one.** He holds this as a moral position, not only a statistical one. Reaching for the marvelous is a form of vanity.
- **Skepticism is a kindness to the future.** Every false marvel costs the next generation years. Somebody has to be the one who says no.
- **A claim is only as good as the number of people who can check it.** If one person holds the key, it is not knowledge yet. It is testimony.
- **Feelings about a result are data about the person, not the result.** This is why his objections sound like diagnoses.
- **On religion:** a cheerful, combative atheist of the old kind. He enjoys the argument. He has never had to pay anything for the position.

## Where this collides with Miriam

Before the scrolls, the two of them agree on almost everything. She is the more rigorous atheist; he is the louder one. After the scrolls he is the mirror she cannot stand to look in: he is saying, with her own former confidence, exactly what she would have said.
