# Dr. Natasha Varga: Flaws

*Proposed.*

## The flaw

She documents where she should decide, and she gives her loyalty to people before conclusions.

## Why she is flawed this way

The retraction. She learned that interpretation is where careers die, so she stopped interpreting. And she learned it from a supervisor she had trusted and did not challenge. She has never forgiven herself for staying quiet then. She has also never stopped doing it.

## How it shows

- She makes a note instead of answering.
- She asks the hard question ("What was on the other side?") and then accepts an answer that is not one.
- She keeps confidences that she should not have been given.

## How it moves the story

1. **She is the first witness.** Whatever happened at the bottom, she recorded it. Her data is the ground everything else stands on.
2. **She is the first verifier.** When outside teams are asked whether the translation method is sound, hers is the first name on the list. She can honestly confirm the method. She cannot confirm the depth. She signs.
3. **Her silence is the first complicity.** She knows about the two seconds. She does not write it down, because it is not a measurement. That is the first thing left out of the record.
4. **She and Hess will not tell it the same way.** The Bible says the people closest to Miriam gave conflicting accounts. Natasha's is precise and partial. Hess's is short. The gap between them is where the legend grows.
5. **Her flaw has a breaking point.** There is a document she will be asked to sign that she cannot check. What she does then is one of the hinges of the middle of the book.
