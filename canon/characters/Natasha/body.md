# Dr. Natasha Varga: Body

*Proposed, except where marked.*

- **Age 37.** Small, wiry, strong for her size.
- **Right ear:** partial hearing loss from a pressure injury on a dive in 2069. She leans toward whoever is speaking. Chapter two already has her leaning forward where others would shout; this is why.
- **Stomach:** seasick for the first three days of every voyage, without complaint.
- **Hands:** cold, always. Steady, always. Her annotations are legible under any conditions.
- **Voice:** level under strain *(from the drafts)*.
- **Implant:** standard research grade. It mirrors her instrument annotations as she makes them.

## What this does to her choices

- **She has a second copy.** Whatever is on her tablet is also in the implant's local store. When the tablets are taken, she still holds the spectra. She will have to decide whether to say so.
- In the cabin she sits with her good ear toward Miriam. She hears Miriam better than she hears Hess. If Miriam says something under her breath, Natasha is the one who catches it.
- She is physically at ease at depth in a way Miriam is not. Below, she is the calm one.
