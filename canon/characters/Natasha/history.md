# Dr. Natasha Varga: History

*Natasha is from chapters one and two. Everything here beyond what those chapters show is a proposal.*

**Fixed by the chapters**

- Runs dive sequencing. Patient, exact, repeats herself without complaint.
- Has developed a shorthand with the protagonist over weeks aboard.
- Annotates and organizes during the descent. Leans forward slightly where another person would shout.
- Says, "That's not a gas pocket."

**Proposed**

- **Born** 2038, Budapest. Family moved to Halifax, Nova Scotia, when she was eight. 37 at the dive.
- **Field:** marine geochemistry. Doctorate on how minerals form at hydrothermal vents.
- **Postdoctoral work** on the mineral armor of vent snails. This is why she recognizes a spectrum that "reads like bone": she has spent two years looking at living things that lay mineral down on protein.
- **The retraction.** In 2066 her supervisor published a claim, on her data, that went well past what the data showed. It was retracted in 2068. Her name was second on the paper.
- **Since then** she has worked on contract, survey to survey, and has never again let a sentence leave her that her instrument did not support.
- **Surname** is a placeholder.
