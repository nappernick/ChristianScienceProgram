# Dr. Natasha Varga: Economics

*Proposed.*

- **Income:** a contract scientist's day rate. No institution, no pension, no lab of her own.
- **This contract** ends four months after the dive. She has nothing lined up.
- **Debt:** the tail end of student loans from two countries.
- **Home:** a rented room in Halifax that she sees for three months a year.
- **What she needs:** one result with her name first. It would get her a permanent post. She has been waiting eleven years for it.
- **What the find is, to her:** that result. Three spectra nobody has seen before, a force curve, four hundred frames.
- **What the survey contract says:** all of it belongs to the sponsors.

## What this does to her choices

- When her tablets are taken, it is not data she is losing. It is the way out of contract work.
- She will follow whoever can give her results a future. For a while that is Iyer. Then it is Miriam.
- She has the most to gain from the find being real and public, and the most practice at not saying so.
