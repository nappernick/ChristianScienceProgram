# Dr. Natasha Varga: Philosophy

*Proposed.*

- **The record outlives the interpretation.** Interpretations get retracted. A clean measurement is still good in fifty years.
- **Say what the instrument said.** Nothing more. "That is all I am prepared to say" is a position, not a dodge.
- **You look after the people in the boat.** Loyalty to whoever you are shut in a small space with is close to absolute for her. It is how you survive at sea and she has not examined it further.
- **Other people's conclusions are theirs.** She will not tell someone what to make of her data, and she will not stop them making it.
- **On religion:** a Hungarian grandmother, a First Communion, and nothing since. The word "Jesus" is still in her mouth for moments when no other word is big enough. She would call herself agnostic if pressed and would dislike being pressed.

## Where this collides with Miriam

Natasha gives facts and withholds meaning. Miriam is about to possess a meaning that no one can get from the facts. Natasha can confirm everything except the part that matters, and she will be asked to confirm it anyway.
