# Dr. Natasha Varga: Psychology

*Proposed.*

- **Core wound:** her name on a retracted paper, and the memory of having known at the time that it claimed too much.
- **What she built on it:** a wall between measurement and meaning. She lives on the measurement side.
- **Temperament:** contained. Her voice goes level when something costs her. People who know her watch for the levelness.
- **Under stress:** she organizes. Files, annotations, timestamps.
- **Toward friends:** steady, undemonstrative, and loyal past the point of sense.
- **What she notices:** people. She reads the person beside her as closely as she reads a spectrum, and says as little about it.
- **What she cannot do:** tell someone their conclusion is wrong when she cannot prove it.
- **Blind spot:** she believes that recording a thing accurately is the same as taking responsibility for it.
