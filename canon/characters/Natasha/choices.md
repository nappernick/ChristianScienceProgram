# Dr. Natasha Varga: Choices

*Proposed.*

| Situation | What she does | Because |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Something inexplicable | Measures it. Twice | The record outlives the interpretation |
| Asked what it means | "That is all I am prepared to say" | The retraction |
| The find becomes an artifact | Hands Miriam the handset | It is correct, and she looks after the people in the boat |
| Miriam confides something | Keeps it | Loyalty before conclusions |
| Her data is confiscated | Hands it over, and says nothing about the copy | She has never yet defied an institution |
| Asked to confirm a method | Confirms it, exactly as far as it goes | It is true |
| Asked to confirm a meaning | Hesitates for a long time | This is the wall |
| Asked under oath what Miriam said | Tells the truth, including that she does not know | She will not round up, even for a friend |

## The line she will not cross

She will not put her name to a number she did not measure. Whether she will put it to a sentence she cannot check is the open question the story is going to ask her.
