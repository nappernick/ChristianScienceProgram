# Dr. Edmund Locke: Flaws

*Proposed.*

## The flaw

He cannot be warm to anyone he respects.

## Why he is flawed this way

The anchor stock. He was praised early and it made him careless, and the correction cost him the career he might have had. He decided that approval is what ruins people, and has withheld it from every good student since.

## How it shows

- He marked her as though carefulness were impertinence.
- He recommended her for the best post of her early life and never mentioned it.
- He did not answer her letter.

## How it moves the story

1. **He is the planned dead end.** His name on the request looks like design: someone who knew what was down there sent the one person who could read it. It is not. He is an old man who answered a telephone and remembered one name. The reader should be allowed to suspect him for a long time.
2. **His silence makes the suspicion possible.** If he were a man who explained himself, there would be no mystery. His flaw is what keeps the thread alive.
3. **He is the source of her discipline.** Whenever she holds back a word until she can support it, that is Locke. When, later, she stops holding back, the reader can measure the distance.
4. **He can supply one scene.** If she ever goes to Durham to ask him, what she gets is an irritable man who says he thought she would be good at it, and asks whether she found the cut.
