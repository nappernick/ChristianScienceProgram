# Dr. Edmund Locke: Philosophy

*Proposed.*

- **Excavation is the orderly destruction of a site.** You find a thing where it lies once. Everything after is a report.
- **Before you believe the layer, look for the cut.** Things get into old places by recent routes.
- **A guess is not a finding.** Do not hand it to anyone until it is.
- **Talent is cheap and care is rare.** He distrusts brilliance and rewards patience, grudgingly.
- **Praise spoils people.** He has never congratulated a student and does not answer letters of thanks.
- **On religion:** a churchgoer of the habitual kind. It has never come up.

## What she took from him

Every working habit she has in the field. The suspicion of a tidy layer, the refusal to say "writing" early, repeating a finding in the same words. She took the method and none of the man.

## What he did not teach her

How to carry something alone. His rule is about when to publish evidence. It has nothing to say about a truth that cannot be handed over at all.
