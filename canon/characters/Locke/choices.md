# Dr. Edmund Locke: Choices

*Proposed.*

| Situation | What he does | Because |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A careful student | Marks her harder than the careless ones | Care that came free irritates him |
| Asked for the best person for a hard job | Names her | He knows exactly how good she is |
| She writes to thank him | Does not reply | Praise spoils people |
| An agency rings for a cultural analyst | Gives the one name he remembers | He is the emeritus who answers the telephone |
| Asked later why he sent her | "I thought you would be good at it." Then: "Did you find the cut?" | It is the truth, and the only question that interests him |
| Told what she found | Asks to see the section drawing | He believes the layer after he has looked for the cut |

## The line he will not cross

He will not say a thing is so before he can show it. He broke that once, at thirty-seven, and built the rest of his life on not doing it again.

## Status in the book

Planned as the deliberate dead end (ledger A5, C9). His name on the request should look like design and turn out to be an old man and a telephone.
