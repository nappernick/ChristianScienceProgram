# Dr. Edmund Locke: Body

*Proposed.*

- **Age 81** at the dive. Thin, stooped, still walks to the department.
- **Knees and back:** forty years of trenches. He has not been on a boat since his sixties.
- **Hands:** a draughtsman's. He still draws sections in pencil.
- **Eyes:** good with a glass. He reads offprints and nothing on a screen.
- **Hearing:** poor on the telephone, which he answers anyway.
- **Implant:** none. He was sixty before they were common and saw no reason.

## What this does to his choices

- He cannot travel, so nothing about the survey involves him in person. He is a name on a form.
- He takes calls badly. Whatever the agency asked him, he may have half heard it.
- No implant and no screen means no record of the call on his side. If anyone asks what was said, there is only what he remembers.
