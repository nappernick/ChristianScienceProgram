# Dr. Edmund Locke: Economics

*Proposed.*

- **Income:** a university pension. Modest and sufficient.
- **Home:** a terraced house in Durham, owned for fifty years, full of offprints.
- **Dependents:** none. Never married.
- **Standing:** emeritus. A desk in the department that nobody has asked him to give up.
- **What he gets for a recommendation:** nothing. Agencies ring old departments for names, and nobody is paid.

## What this does to his choices

- He has no stake in the survey, the sponsors, or the find. There is no interest behind his name on the request, which is why looking for one leads nowhere.
- He still answers the department telephone because it is the last thing anyone asks of him.
- He cannot be bought, pressured, or flattered. He can only be visited.
