# Dr. Edmund Locke: History

*Locke is from chapter one. Everything beyond what that chapter shows is a proposal. First name is a placeholder.*

**Fixed by the chapter**

- Taught the protagonist archaeology as an undergraduate.
- Made an unnecessarily clear show of disliking her, for reasons she never understood.
- Put his name on the recommendation that got her onto the Antikythera team at twenty-six.
- Did not contact her again until his name appeared on the request for this survey.

**Proposed**

- **Born** 1994. 81 at the time of the dive. Has not left Durham in a decade.
- **Field:** maritime archaeology. Forty years of shipwrecks, most of them unremarkable.
- **His one large mistake.** In 2031 he announced a wreck as Phoenician on the strength of an inscribed anchor stock. The inscription was a nineteenth-century salvage mark. He withdrew the claim himself, in print, before anyone caught it.
- **What he taught afterward:** look for the cut, and never say the word before you can support it.
- **Why he disliked her:** she was careful by nature. He had become careful by humiliation, and it irritated him to see it come free to someone of twenty.
- **Why he recommended her in 2055:** the Antikythera team wanted someone who could read damaged text and would not overclaim. He knew one.
- **Why his name is on the 2075 request:** the agency asked his old department for a cultural analyst at short notice. He was the emeritus who answered the telephone. He gave the only former student's name he could remember.
