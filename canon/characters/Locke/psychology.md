# Dr. Edmund Locke: Psychology

*Proposed.*

- **Core wound:** a public claim, withdrawn by his own hand. Nobody caught him. He caught himself, and that was worse.
- **What he built on it:** a teaching method made entirely of suspicion.
- **Temperament:** dry, impatient, exact.
- **Toward good students:** colder than toward bad ones. A bad student cannot disappoint him.
- **Under stress:** he goes to the evidence and stays there.
- **Pride:** in having been wrong properly, once, and never since.
- **Loneliness:** considerable, and unexamined.
- **What he cannot do:** say that he is pleased.
- **Blind spot:** he believes his students know what he thinks of them.
