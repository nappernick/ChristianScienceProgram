# Miriam Calder: Economics

## Fixed by the Bible

- Brilliant, respected, well paid.
- Works in the upper tier of academic, corporate and government contracts: training language models, consulting for intelligence agencies on communications analysis, classified translation.
- Holds a security clearance, and knows how people with clearances talk to each other.
- During the secular years she learned how institutions run, how funding works, and how information is controlled.

## Proposed

- **Why someone at her level is on a routine survey for a cultural analyst's fee.** Her last classified contract ended with a mandatory ninety-day stand-down, during which she may not take communications work. A month at sea is a way to wait it out. Locke's name on the request made her curious enough to say yes.
- **Assets:** an apartment she owns outright, in a city not yet named. Savings. No debts.
- **Dependents:** none known. The Bible is silent on a partner or family.
- **What she needs from this expedition:** nothing.

## What this does to her choices

- **She cannot be pressured through money.** In that one respect she is like Dunmore.
- She knows exactly what Carrow is doing when he takes the records, because she has sat on his side of that table.
- Her clearance means she has a way to reach people in government that nobody else aboard has. It is the first rung of the path the Bible leaves open: how a linguist becomes a head of state.
- She came aboard needing nothing. Within a week she will need one thing completely.
