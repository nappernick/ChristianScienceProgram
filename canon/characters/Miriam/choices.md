# Miriam Calder: Choices

How she decides at the time of the dive, and where the Bible says it changes.

## At the dive

| Situation | What she does | Because |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A finding | States it exactly, and repeats it in the same words | A scholar's discipline |
| A word that cannot be taken back | Keeps it until she can support it | She has seen careers end on one |
| A person with authority over the decision | Works out what they need and offers it | She sees mechanisms |
| A friend asks what she was thinking | Tells her | Her instinct is to share |
| The object at risk against people at risk | Weighs them, and the scale does not tip at once | The first sign of what the Bible describes |
| A rule she holds | Keeps it, until the object is on the other side | The same |

## How it changes, by the Bible

| Stage | The shift |
| --- | --- |
| The translation | Certainty returns, pointed the other way |
| The failed attempts to share | She stops expecting to be followed. Sharing becomes selective, then strategic |
| The tour | One glimpse of the private self per person. Then closed |
| The golden age | She plans in decades and is, briefly, happy |
| The stall | The old doubt comes back |
| The first violence | An irreversible act to make the doubt stop |
| After | Each act is laid over the last. The question is never asked again |

## The line she will not cross, at the dive

She will not state a thing she does not believe. Later, the Bible says, the line between translating honestly and managing what is known becomes hard to find. It should be possible to point to the scene where it first blurs.
