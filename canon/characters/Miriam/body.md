# Miriam Calder: Body

## Fixed by the chapters

- The pressure chamber leaves her foggy. She cannot tell whether she needs a doctor or coffee.
- Her hands check her equipment while she is listening to something else.
- She carries a paper notebook in the chest pocket of her suit and can feel its weight against her sternum.
- She has done enough dives to follow the shape of a prep by habit.

## Proposed

- **Age 46** at the dive.
- **The paper habit** comes from classified work. Secure rooms bar devices. For fifteen years the only notes she was allowed to take out were the ones she could make by hand and have reviewed.
- **Implant:** she has one. It is dormant, under the terms of her clearance, whenever she is outside an approved facility. At sea she is one of the few people aboard with nothing recording for her.
- **Cold:** she feels it more than Natasha or Hess. Two pairs of socks.
- **Sleep:** adequate, until the dive.
- **Hands:** steady, except once, after the nine seconds.
- **Languages in the mouth:** Koine Greek, Hebrew and church Latin, unused aloud for twenty years. They surface without permission.

## What this does to her choices

- **The notebook is the only private record of the dive.** Everything else was logged by a machine that belongs to someone. That is why it becomes a relic, and why it stays hers.
- With no implant running, what she saw on the cylinder or the scrolls is in her head and on paper. From the first day, her knowledge is harder to copy than anyone else's.
- The old languages coming up unbidden is the body getting there before the mind does.
