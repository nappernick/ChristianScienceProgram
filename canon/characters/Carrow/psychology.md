# Simon Carrow: Psychology

- **Where he comes from:** a naval family. Nine schools before he was sixteen. He learned to enter a room, say nothing, and work out who mattered.
- **What that built:** a man who listens first and trusts structures over people.
- **Control is how he stays calm.** A situation with a narrow circle and a clear record is one he can sleep through. He rarely sleeps.
- **Friends:** none close. He does not experience this as a lack.
- **His son** is the one place the method fails. A ten-year-old cannot be managed by narrowing the circle.
- **Under stress:** quieter and more polite.
- **What he respects:** competence, discretion, and people who do not need things explained.
- **What he cannot read:** someone acting from conviction. He looks for the interest behind it and, finding none, assumes it is well hidden.
- **Blind spot:** he thinks a thing is under control once he holds the records.
