## What is running out

- **Days.** A stranger's vessel has seen the site and gone to report.
- **His son's childhood.** Ten now. The shore posting is worth less each year it is delayed.

## Where he is in his life

Mid-career, good at a narrow thing, and aware that this is the largest version of it he will ever be handed. He believes that if he handles it the way he handled the others, it will end the same way.
