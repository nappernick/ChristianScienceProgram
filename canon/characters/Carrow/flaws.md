# Simon Carrow: Flaws

## The flaw

He sees everything as an asset to be held, and he cannot recognize a person who is not playing his game until they have beaten him at it.

## Why he is flawed this way

A career of listening taught him that people give themselves away constantly and that the advantage goes to whoever is quiet. It worked every time. He has never met a find that was bigger than the organizations fighting over it, so he has no way to see that this one is.

## How it shows

- He takes the recorder and the tablets and believes he now has the find.
- He misreads Miriam's openness as weakness.
- He plans in days and weeks. The object was built to wait two hundred thousand years.

## How it moves the story

1. **He teaches Miriam information control by example.** Your Bible says she learned, in her secular years, how information is controlled. Carrow is where she watches it done to her own discovery, in an afternoon, by a professional.
2. **He gives her the first reason to keep something back.** Before him, she has no motive to withhold. After him, everything about the day belongs to four companies and a government, except what is in her head.
3. **He starts the clock.** His reading of the other mapper ("it went to tell someone") turns a patient excavation into a race. Miriam's first break with her own principles, lifting the object before there is a plan, happens under his pressure.
4. **He is the first of the professionals who do not see what she is.** Your Bible says the political class dismisses her until it is too late. He is the prototype, at small scale.
