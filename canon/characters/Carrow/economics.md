# Simon Carrow: Economics

- **Employer:** a risk committee representing four companies and one government, who agree on almost nothing.
- **Pay:** salary, plus a bonus that pays out on "asset retention events." Keeping a find exclusive for its first year is the largest such event there is.
- **Equity:** options in one of the four companies. He would prefer that one to come out ahead and is careful never to act on it.
- **Obligations:** an ex-wife, shared custody, school fees.
- **What he wants:** a shore posting, so that he sees his son more than five months a year. A find this size, handled well, buys that.

## What this does to his choices

- **He serves five masters and therefore none.** The only position safe from all of them is that nobody knows anything yet. Secrecy is not just his temperament. It is the one policy that cannot be held against him.
- He is being rewarded for delay, in a situation where the object's value does not decay and everyone else's patience will.
- He will sequester records, not destroy them. Destroyed records cannot be produced later to show he did his job.
- When the secret gets out, his first need is someone to have been responsible.
