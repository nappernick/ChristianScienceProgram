# Simon Carrow: Philosophy

- **Information is a resource with a half-life.** Its value decays as more people hold it. The job is to slow the decay until your side has used it.
- **Whoever knows first, owns.** Not legally. Practically.
- **Secrecy is a form of stewardship.** He believes this sincerely. Things released before anyone is ready do damage, and he has seen it.
- **Everyone is playing.** He assumes every person in a room is managing what they reveal. Scientists who simply say what they found strike him as either naive or very good.
- **On religion:** no position and no interest. If the object turns out to be a bible, that is a fact about its market.

## Where this collides with Miriam

He thinks she is naive. She is, on the day of the dive. But she learns his whole method by watching him use it once, and she has something he will never have, which is that she is the only one who can read the asset.
