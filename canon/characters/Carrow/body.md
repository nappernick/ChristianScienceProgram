# Simon Carrow: Body

- **Age 44.** Medium height, fit, forgettable. People describe him afterward by his clothes.
- **Ears:** trained. Twelve years of listening to hydrophone arrays. He can pick a twelve-kilohertz survey chirp out of ship noise without an instrument, and did.
- **Stomach:** never seasick. He eats early because the mess is empty.
- **Implant:** high-grade and employer-issued. It logs his communications for the committee. He is watched as closely as he watches.
- **Sleep:** four hours, broken.
- **Manner:** still. He does not fidget, gesture, or raise his voice.

## What this does to his choices

- He heard the other vessel before the ship's own array flagged it. His instincts about sound are the best aboard and should be trusted in the text.
- Because his own implant reports on him, he says nothing he would not want read back. His speech is short for that reason.
- He has no private channel. If he ever wants to act for himself, he will have to do it in person, off the record, and the reader will know it matters.
