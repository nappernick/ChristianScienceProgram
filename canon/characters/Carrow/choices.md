# Simon Carrow: Choices

| Situation | What he does | Because |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Something is found | Narrows the circle, takes the records, waits | It has worked every time |
| A scientist objects | Lets them finish, then repeats the instruction | Arguing widens the circle |
| A rival appears | Moves faster and tells fewer people | Whoever knows first, owns |
| Asked what he wants | Asks a question back | His implant is logging |
| Someone idealistic | Looks for their angle, finds none, and discounts them | He cannot read conviction |
| The secret gets out | Cuts the loss and finds who was responsible | The committee will want a name |
| Miriam turns out to be the only reader | Tries to put her under contract | An asset with one key holder is managed through the key holder |
| She refuses | Has no next move | He has never met a find bigger than its owners |

## The line he will not cross

He will not destroy evidence and he will not harm anyone physically. He is a custodian. What he does to people, he does with paperwork.
