# Simon Carrow: History

*The sponsors' representative aboard the 2075 survey. Appears in Draft 2 only; held back from Draft 3 for a later chapter. First name is a placeholder.*

- **Born** 2031. 44 at the time of the dive.
- **First career:** naval acoustic intelligence. He spent his twenties listening to the ocean for a living and knows precisely how far a sound carries and who is likely to hear it.
- **Second career:** security and "asset continuity" for a maritime-resources consortium. In practice he is the person sent when a survey finds something awkward.
- **Prior finds.** A wreck whose cargo embarrassed a government. A rival's listening device on a cable. A mineral deposit that had to stay unannounced for eleven months. He has handled each one the same way: narrow the circle, control the record, wait.
- **Aboard.** He eats early and alone and has said perhaps ten words to Miriam in a month. She has taken him for an accountant.
- **At the dive.** He is the one who understands that the acoustic link is a loudspeaker, and who hears the other mapper before anyone thinks to worry about it.

## Proposed arc

- He is the first professional to try to own the find, and the first to underestimate Miriam.
- He is a candidate to grow into one of your inner-ring disciples. The Financial Operator fits best: the cold one who does not care about the theology and intends to be on the winning side.
