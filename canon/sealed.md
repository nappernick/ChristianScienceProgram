# Sealed

The author's private answers to the questions the book keeps open. Collaborators write toward these and never state them in prose.

Each question is in one of three states. The difference between the second and third matters: a draft can lean on something open by design, and must not lean on something not yet decided.

- **Decided:** the author knows. The reader may or may not be told.
- **Open by design:** the author has chosen never to settle it, on the page or off.
- **Not yet decided:** needs an answer before the chapters that touch it.
