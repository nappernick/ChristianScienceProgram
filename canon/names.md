## Shared names, on purpose

- **Wren:** the submersible, then a girl's name among the Wrights, then the Year 304 point of view.
- **Hess:** the pilot, then a common boy's name, then Wren's brother and this year's Reader.
- **Natasha:** the scientist, then the commonest girl's name of the departure generation.
- **Miriam:** withdrawn from ordinary use after the war. Nobody aboard is called Miriam.

A scene with two Hesses in it needs a way to tell them apart that is not the name.
