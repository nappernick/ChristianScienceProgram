## Customs still without a lineage

The `canon/ship-era/` files describe many: zero-gravity baptism, burial in light, the Stations, the prayer hours, the guild and ecclesial ranks. None has been traced to a 2075 fact. Each needs a row here before a scene uses it.

## The text that does not exist yet

The **Reading** itself, the full Vigil with the Reader's lines and the people's replies, is quoted by the lore and has never been written out. It should be assembled from chapters 2 and 3 once those are final, and kept in this folder.
