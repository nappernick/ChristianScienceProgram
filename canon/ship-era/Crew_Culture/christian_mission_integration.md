# Christian Mission Integration Framework

*Research from initial planning session*

## Core Distinction

**"Christians in space" vs "A Christian Space Program"**

This isn't secular space exploration with religious people - this is a **fundamentally Christian mission** where theology shapes every decision.

---

## What "Finding God" Means Theologically

### Not Metaphorical
- **Literally finding God** somewhere in the cosmos
- Actual location or encounter
- Physical journey toward divine presence
- Not enlightenment - actual meeting

### Theological Precedents
- Jacob's Ladder (Genesis 28) - God in specific place
- Moses and Burning Bush (Exodus 3) - God localized
- Temple theology - God dwelling in specific location
- Incarnation - God in specific body at specific time

### Possible Destinations
- **Dyson Sphere around galactic core** - God's throne?
- **Omega Point** - convergence of all reality
- **New Jerusalem** - literal structure in space (Revelation 21)
- **The place where Word became flesh** - cosmic Bethlehem

### Questions This Raises
- Is God truly omnipresent if findable in one place?
- Does finding God mean reaching God, or God revealing Himself?
- What happens after arrival?

## Christian Governance and Decision-Making

### Discernment vs Consensus

**Not:** "What do we think should be done?"

**But:** "What is God already doing that we should join?"

**Scriptural Model:**
- Acts 15:28 - "It seemed good to the Holy Spirit and to us"
- Not majority vote
- Not autocratic command
- Communal listening for divine will

### The Process

1. **Prayer and Silence**
   - Extended periods seeking God's will
   - Fasting may accompany major decisions
   - Individual and communal prayer

2. **Scripture and Tradition**
   - What do Scriptures say?
   - How have Christians historically approached this?
   - Wisdom of church fathers

3. **Reason and Experience**
   - What do we know technically?
   - What has past experience taught?
   - Science and faith integrated

4. **Communal Discernment**
   - Sharing what each person senses
   - Looking for unity of Spirit
   - Dissent taken seriously (could be prophetic)

5. **Testing**
   - Does it produce fruit of Spirit? (Galatians 5:22-23)
   - Does it build up community?
   - Does it align with character of Christ?

### When Discernment Is Unclear
- Wait (patience as spiritual discipline)
- Seek outside counsel (transmission to Earth/other ships)
- Move forward in faith with humility
- Willingness to change course if wrong

## Christian Ethics Shaping Practical Decisions

### Alien Contact

**First Contact Protocol:**

Not: "How do we protect ourselves?"

But: "How do we love our neighbor who happens to be alien?"

**Questions:**
- Do they have souls?
- Are they fallen? Redeemed?
- What do we owe them?
- How do we share Gospel without imperialism?

**Principles:**
- Dignity of all conscious beings (imago Dei possibly universal?)
- Non-violence unless absolute necessity
- Transparency, not deception
- Seeking their good, not our advantage

### Resource Decisions

**Mining, Harvesting, Terraforming:**

Not: "Is it profitable/necessary?"

But: "Are we stewarding Creation or exploiting it?"

**Questions:**
- Genesis 2:15 - "work it and take care of it"
- Does non-Earth creation deserve Sabbath rest?
- Can we take life (alien ecosystems) even if not sentient?
- What do we owe to planets we visit?

**Principles:**
- Sustainability over extraction
- Gratitude and reverence
- Minimizing harm
- Creation care as worship

### Colonization Ethics

**Settlement of New Worlds:**

Not: "Can we survive here?"

But: "Should we be here? Are we invited?"

**Questions:**
- Right of presence vs right of conquest
- What if world has primitive life? Intelligent life?
- Are we expanding Kingdom or building empire?
- Difference between mission and colonialism

**Principles:**
- Seeking permission (from God, from creation, from inhabitants)
- Co-creation, not domination
- Building communities, not extracting resources
- Long-term commitment, not exploitation

## Worship and Sacraments Integration

### Daily Operations

**Everything as liturgy:**
- Engineering checks as prayer
- Navigation as pilgrimage
- Meals as communion (small-c)
- Sleep as sabbath rest

**Specific Practices:**
- Morning prayer before bridge duty
- Blessing tools before use
- Prayers of protection before EVA
- Gratitude before and after work shifts

### Sacramental Life

**Essential Sacraments Maintained:**
- Baptism (see zero-G adaptations)
- Eucharist (daily or weekly)
- Marriage
- Confession/Reconciliation
- Anointing of sick/dying
- Ordination (can new priests be ordained aboard?)
- Confirmation (for children coming of age)

### Scripture Integration

**Not just chapel time:**
- Scripture memorization during long watches
- Lectio Divina (meditative reading)
- Biblical names for discoveries (planets, stars, species)
- Navigation by psalm and prophecy (spiritual and literal)

- Universal atonement

- Incarnation
- Resurrection
- Salvation through Christ
- Love of God and neighbor

- Forms of worship in new contexts

## Difference from Secular Space Exploration

| Secular Program | Christian Space Program |
|----------------|------------------------|
| Discover and return | Seek and remain |
| Extract resources | Steward creation |
| Survive and expand | Worship and serve |
| Command structure | Servant leadership |
| Individual achievement | Community formation |
| Progress and profit | Pilgrimage and transformation |
| Conquer unknown | Meet the Other with reverence |

## Integration into Every System

**Navigation:** Pilgrimage language, prayer over course plotting

**Engineering:** Stewardship of resources, gratitude for function

**Science:** Wonder and worship, studying God's creation

**Medicine:** Healing as sacred ministry, dignity of patients

**Food Production:** Gardens as Eden, meals as fellowship

**Communication:** Truth-telling, building up, no gossip

**Conflict:** Reconciliation, forgiveness, restoration

## Visual Design Implications

- Beauty as offering to God
