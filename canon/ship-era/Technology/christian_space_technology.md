# Christian Space Technology - Theology Driving Innovation

> "Whatever you do, work at it with all your heart, as working for the Lord" - Colossians 3:23

---

## Core Principle

Technology development is **stewardship of Creation** - using God-given minds to fulfill the calling to explore His universe.

Every technology asks: **"How does this serve the mission and honor the Creator?"**

## Propulsion Systems

### Fusion Drives - Harnessing Stellar Fire

**Theological Basis:**
- Genesis 1:14-18 - God created stars "for signs and seasons"
- We harness the same process that powers stars
- Mini-sun aboard our ships - bringing light into darkness
- Power source that reflects divine creative power

**Technical Approach:**
- Deuterium-Helium3 fusion
- Clean, sustainable, abundant
- No weapons applications - purely peaceful use
- Sacred numerology in reactor design (8-fold symmetry)

**Visual Elements:**
- Reactor core glowing like a captive star
- Light emanating from drive section
- Cross-shaped heat radiators
- Fusion as metaphor for Trinity (many becoming one)

### Ion Drives - Patient, Persistent Journey

**Theological Basis:**
- The journey matters as much as destination
- Patience as virtue (James 1:4)
- Slow, steady, faithful acceleration
- Marathon, not sprint

**Technical Approach:**
- High efficiency, low thrust
- Years of continuous operation
- Minimal fuel requirements
- Reliance on solar power near stars

**Visual Elements:**
- Gentle blue glow from ion exhaust
- Arrays of solar panels like wings
- Long, slow acceleration curves
- Beauty in gradual progress

## Technology Development Principles

### Innovation Framework

**Questions Before Development:**
1. Does this serve the mission to find God?
2. Does this honor human dignity?
3. Does this steward resources responsibly?
4. Can this be sustained long-term?
5. What are unintended consequences?
6. Does this build community or isolate?

**What We Will Not Build:**
- Weapons of mass destruction
- Technologies that dehumanize
- Unsustainable resource extraction
- Anything that requires deception
- Enhancement beyond healing
- Autonomous moral agents (AI with "souls")

**What We Will Build:**
- Life-sustaining systems
- Communication and communion tools
- Sustainable propulsion
- Medical healing technology
- Beauty and wonder
- Tools that free humans for higher calling
