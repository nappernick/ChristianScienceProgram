# Spacecraft Designs - Christian Space Program

*Research from initial planning session*

## Design Philosophy

**Sacred geometry and cathedral architecture principles fundamentally shaping the engineering** - not superficial Christian decoration, but theological concepts driving the actual structure and function.

## Key Design Principles

### Cruciform (Cross) Configurations
- Ships designed in cross shapes where the structure serves both symbolic AND engineering purposes
- The form follows both faith and function

### Golden Ratio (Divine Proportion)
- Used throughout ship dimensions
- Mathematical harmony reflecting divine order

### Rose Window Habitat Rings
- Rotating habitat rings designed like Notre Dame's rose windows
- Rotation provides gravity while symbolizing sacred architecture
- Light patterns through the "petals" create moving mandalas

### East-Facing Orientation
- Ships oriented toward their destination star (toward "resurrection")
- Navigation and theology aligned

### Sacred Numerology in Engineering
- **8 engines** = resurrection/new beginning (8th day)
- **12 crew members** = the apostles
- **6 drives** = creation (6 days)
- Numbers carry meaning beyond mere function

## Ship Classes

### Guardian-Class
- **Core Tension**: Wrestles with pacifist ideals vs. protective duty
- "Shepherd's staff, not seeking wolves"
- Defensive capabilities designed with reluctance and theological reflection
- Questions: When is force justified? How do we protect without becoming warriors?

## Visual Elements to Design

1. **Exterior Hull Designs**
   - Cruciform configurations from multiple angles
   - Golden ratio proportions visible in dimensions
   - Rose window structures

2. **Interior Spaces**
   - Cathedral-like corridors
   - Sacred geometry in floor plans
   - Integration of worship spaces into engineering

3. **Navigation & Bridge**
   - East-facing orientation systems
   - Integration of theological symbols into functional displays

4. **Engine Arrays**
   - 8-engine configurations
   - Visual representation of power as divine energy

## Notes for Image Generation

- Focus on how Christian theology shapes the actual engineering, not decoration
- Ships should look functional first, but their function follows theological principles
- Avoid superficial crosses - instead show how sacred geometry creates the structure
- Consider lighting, shadows, and how the ships interact with starlight
