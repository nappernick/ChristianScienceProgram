# Outline

## The decision this file is waiting on

The Bible runs from 2075 to the galactic center. The character folders cover one week in 2075 and one year aboard the ship. Nothing yet says **where this book stops**. Three workable answers:

| Option | 2075 line ends at | Year 304 line ends at | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **One book, narrow** | The moment she first lets a false version stand, or first withholds | Wren speaks from the Reader's place | The war, the disciples and the golden age stay offstage, known only as the ship's history. Smallest cast. Finishable |
| **One book, wide** | The first violence | The same | Needs the seven disciples, the tour, China. Two or three times the length |
| **Several books** | Book one as "narrow"; later books take the tour, the golden age, the war | Wren's year spans all of them, or each book gets its own ship generation | Each book must still end |

Recommendation: **narrow**. It uses everything already built, the two lines cross where the ensemble file says they should, and the Bible's later parts work harder as fossils than as scenes.
