# Notes: Chapter 3, draft 3

Draft 3 is the synthesis of two earlier drafts: a quiet one (the ship rite cut against the dive, nothing goes wrong) and an intense one (a countdown, sworn testimony, a deck scene with Carrow). Drafts 1 and 2 are not in this repository.

## Inventions listed with the draft

- Her name. Miriam Calder throughout. Chapters one and two still say Mariana.
- Her field. A computational linguist who trained first as an archaeologist. In Draft 1 her Antikythera work at twenty-six is on newly recovered fragments, as the inscriptions specialist, because the mechanism itself came up in 1901.
- New people. Iyer (chief scientist, owns the date), Dunmore (petrologist, the skeptic), and in Draft 2 only, Carrow (the sponsors’ man).
- Marks on the cylinder. Your Bible puts the writing on the scrolls inside. I put a band of signs on the container that opens with a one and then the primes.
- What the cylinder is made of. A biomineral that “reads like bone.” That squares the Bible’s “stone cylinder” with chapter two’s “neither rock nor metal.”
- A fix chapter two needs. It says finer grain means slower cooling. In real basalt it is the reverse. Natasha now says “fast and even, you don’t get both,” which keeps your idea and corrects the physics.
- The ship. Year 304, the hub chapel, the Vigil, the Pause, Wren, Sister Perpetua, and the Notebook as a relic are all mine.
- The mapper. In Drafts 2 and 3 someone else’s survey drone has seen the site. That starts a clock for chapter four.
