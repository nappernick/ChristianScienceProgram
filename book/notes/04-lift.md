## Tests set before any draft was written

- Passive: the object never acts. Water, rock and people do the work.
- Clock: 70 minutes down, 70 up, every hour accounted for.
- Arc: Miriam shares by instinct and lies to nobody.
- Hostile reader: masses, buoyancy, compressibility, snatch loads and recovery limits survive a calculator.
- Linguist: her decisive act could not have been done by Dunmore or Natasha.
- Length: at least 6,500 words.
- Added by the review: no event depends on luck arriving on cue; "It's writing" costs her something she does not notice.

## Plan 1: The Carry

One 2075 day in clock-headed scenes, then a ship coda. Miriam gives Dunmore her seat, so the lift reaches her in the control van as a tension reading. Flat sea; the lid comes off clean. Tobias carries the thing eleven steps. The seam widens through the afternoon. She counts for an hour, finds the correction, says "It's writing." Dunmore writes his dissent in her notebook. Wren is handed the Reader's book.

## Plan 2: The Wire

2075 only, cut by bridge-log lines. A recovery limit of 2.5 m of sea at 13:00. Miriam is in the vehicle. The swell outruns the heave compensator, the line takes a 10.4-tonne snatch, and the plate is put down propped on one corner. Hess will not say; she tells him to slide it out. Carrow orders it crated unopened; the seam defeats the order; he seals the lab. Dunmore takes her in through the rock-store hatch. She finds the correction; Carrow is at the door; she gives him four names.

Draft 1 does best: the lid coming off as a number; "We are not opening it. It is opening"; the hour of counting and the agreement about what will not do; the dissent in the notebook; the Reader's book.

Draft 2 does best: Hess's speech that the thing is safe where it lies; her call, the object weighed against itself; no drawing made; "It is why you were hired. It is also why not"; the four names and "He asked."

## Plan 3: One Night

Kept from Draft 2: forecast and recovery limit, snatch load, propped plate, Hess's speech, her call, no drawing, Carrow's argument, the four names. Kept from Draft 1: the lid as a number, the carry at a watchable pace, "It is opening," the full count, the dissent, the Reader's book.

Changed: she offers Dunmore the seat and he refuses it. The plate is still standing when they leave. Iyer reads clause fourteen exactly ("at the rail"), which gives one lawful night and removes the break-in. The sea sets the clock but does not rush the count. Carrow stays in the room and schemes at nothing. The other vessel is named once. Tobias sends a message in plain sight.
