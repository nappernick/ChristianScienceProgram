## Structure

1. The ships' five accounts of her (far-timeline voice, about 200 words).
2. 02:20, dry lab. The fourth run comes off the instrument. Dunmore's clipboard. Carrow's request. Iyer asks what she is for.
3. 03:10, her cabin. The midnight sonar pass. No fissure above the object. Locke and Antikythera. The message she deletes.
4. 04:40, prep room. She shows Natasha the notebook. The fissure survey goes on the dive plan.
5. 05:40, aft deck. Tobias, Hess, Iyer. The hatch.

## Kept from v3

Natasha repeating the sequence without complaint. The doctor-or-coffee line. "The agitation of a system encountering an input it can't process." "You don't date a shipwreck by dating the seafloor." Locke's unexplained dislike. The particular silence. "Ready when you are." "It was time to go down."

## Changed from v3

- The opening sentences. "Natasha was speaking to her" and the rain simile are gone; the drifting-off beat moves to 04:40.
- The dinner argument is no longer summarized. It happens live, at night, with the result arriving.
- "Pressure chamber" is now a seasickness patch. A crewed submersible stays at surface pressure.
- Antikythera: the mechanism is raised in 1901; she reads fragments found in the fifties.
- "Before the first hominid" is gone. The date is set against the oldest writing.
- "When to share and when to wait" is scoped to findings.

## Tests

- **Trace:** Iyer reruns at night (psychology), hands in sleeves (body), hands over the call in advance (choices). Dunmore checks alone and will not say it (psychology, choices). Carrow narrows the circle (choices). Natasha records and will not interpret, turns her good ear (philosophy, body). Tobias sings, prays, and already knows (psychology, body). Hess says one thing (psychology).
- **Swap:** the words people will not use is something only she would notice. The missing cut is something only she would look for.
- **Hostile reader:** argon dating needs neutron irradiation; a source is aboard. Step heating and plateau are as practiced. Age figures carry errors.
- **Clock:** the dive at six.
- **Check tool:** no "Mariana." One "the particular" (the silence, kept on purpose).

## Problems this draft creates

- Hess speaks two sentences on deck and the narration calls it more than usual. Chapter 2 makes the same joke with "Twenty minutes," and the Vigil depends on that one. One of them should go.
- Chapter 2 opens as if Hess were new and as if nothing had been planned. It now needs the fissure survey first, and "several dives" should be her fifth.
- The opening voice knows the future. See ledger B18.
