# Christian Space Program - Design Documentation

> **Mission Statement**: To literally find God somewhere in the cosmos, with Christianity shaping every aspect of the journey - not as decoration, but as the fundamental DNA of the program.

## Overview

This repository contains the comprehensive research and design documentation for the Christian Space Program - a sci-fi universe where **theology drives technology**, not the other way around.

**Core Principle**: This isn't "sci-fi with crosses on it" - this is a fundamentally Christian mission where faith shapes every decision, design, and interaction.

## Key Concepts

### Sacred Geometry in Engineering
Ships aren't decorated with crosses - they're **built as crosses**. Golden ratio, sacred numerology (8 engines = resurrection, 12 crew = apostles), cathedral architecture principles fundamentally shaping engineering.

### Exotheology
Serious theological wrestling with alien encounters:
- Can Christ's atonement save aliens?
- Do energy beings need resurrection?
- Is individual consciousness necessary for salvation?

### Pilgrimage, Not Exploration
This isn't exploration for resources or knowledge - it's a **literal journey to find God** at a physical location in the cosmos.

### Monastic Community Structure
Not military hierarchy - **ecclesial governance** based on discernment, servant leadership, and communal prayer. Multi-generational communities living Rule of Benedict in space.

### Everything as Liturgy
No separation between sacred and secular:
- Engineering as prayer
- Navigation as pilgrimage
- Meals as fellowship
- Work and prayer (Ora et Labora)

## Design Philosophy

Every element should answer: **"How does Christianity fundamentally shape this?"**

Not:
- ❌ "Add a cross to the spaceship"
- ❌ "Put a Bible in the scene"
- ❌ "Generic religious characters"

Instead:
- ✅ "How does sacred geometry determine hull structure?"
- ✅ "How do sacraments work in zero-G?"
- ✅ "What theological questions would aliens ask?"
