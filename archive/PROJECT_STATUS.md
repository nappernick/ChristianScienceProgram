## Core Design Principles Established

### 1. **The Cross is Central** ✝️
- Every ship MUST be obviously cross-shaped
- Not subtle, not metaphorical - **LITERAL GIANT CROSSES**
- Cross on every spacesuit
- Cross in all architecture
- Visual witness to Christ crucified

### 2. **Realism Over Fantasy**
- Grounded in actual physics
- Achievable technology (fusion, ion drives, closed-loop life support)
- Photorealistic visual style
- **Goal: Inspire Christians that this is REAL possibility**

### 3. **Theology Drives Technology**
- Not "sci-fi with crosses slapped on"
- Christianity shapes every design decision
- Sacred geometry actually determines engineering
- Exotheology seriously wrestled with

### 4. **Multi-Generational Mission**
- Not individuals on rotation - **families and communities**
- Monastic structures in space
- Children born in deep space
- Pilgrimage lasting centuries

### 5. **Beauty as Offering**
- Everything made with reverence
- Quality matters - for God's glory
- Photorealistic, cinematic, beautiful
- Inspiring wonder and worship

## Phase 3: Interactive Experience (FUTURE)

### Simulator/Game Concept

#### Core Design Philosophy
- **Not a combat game** - exploration and community simulation
- **Educational and inspirational** - teach Christian space vision
- **Contemplative pacing** - not action-oriented
- **Theological depth** - real questions, real choices

#### Possible Platforms

**Option A: VR Experience**
- **Pros:** Immersive, inspiring presence, scale of space
- **Cons:** Expensive equipment, smaller audience
- **Best for:** Chapel experiences, EVA work, alien encounters
- **Tools:** Unity + XR Toolkit, Unreal Engine 5 VR

**Option B: Desktop Sim (Asteroid/KSP-style)**
- **Pros:** Accessible, complex systems possible, moddable
- **Cons:** Less immersive, steeper learning curve
- **Best for:** Ship design, mission planning, long-term strategy
- **Tools:** Unity, Unreal Engine, custom engine

**Option C: Narrative Experience (Walking Sim)**
- **Pros:** Story-focused, accessible, beautiful, contemplative
- **Cons:** Less "gameplay," might feel limited
- **Best for:** Theological exploration, character stories
- **Tools:** Unreal Engine 5 (Nanite/Lumen), Unity

**Option D: Hybrid Approach** ⭐ **RECOMMENDED**
- Story-driven narrative core (exploration, discovery)
- Light simulation elements (ship management, prayer cycles)
- VR-optional (plays in desktop or VR)
- Episodic releases (funded by earlier episodes)

#### Core Gameplay Loops

**Daily Rhythm:**
1. Wake for Lauds (morning prayer)
2. Breakfast and assignments
3. Work shift (engineering, gardens, research)
4. Prayer hours throughout day
5. Community meals
6. Vespers (evening prayer)
7. Personal time, relationships
8. Compline (night prayer)

**Long-Term Arcs:**
- **Journey:** Traveling toward destination (years)
- **Community:** Relationships, conflicts, reconciliation
- **Discovery:** New worlds, aliens, phenomena
- **Faith:** Theological questions, doubt and belief
- **Generations:** Children born, elders dying, continuation

**Player Choices:**
- Not moral binary (good/evil)
- Christian discernment (what is God doing?)
- Community impact (affect whole crew)
- Long-term consequences (decades later)

#### Key Features

**Theological Integration:**
- Prayer as actual gameplay mechanic (not cutscene)
- Discernment process for major decisions
- Scripture reading integrated
- Sacraments as meaningful events
- Exotheology dialogues with aliens

**Visual Beauty:**
- Photorealistic graphics (Unreal Engine 5)
- Chiaroscuro lighting
- Cross-shaped ships always visible
- Cathedral-like interiors
- Contemplative pacing allows appreciation

**Multiplayer Possibility:**
- Co-op community management
- Different crew members (different roles)
- Shared discernment (prayer together)
- Asynchronous "decades apart" communication

#### Development Roadmap

**Prototype (3-6 months):**
- Single chapel space (explorable)
- Zero-G baptism scene
- Prayer mechanic prototype
- Cross-ship exterior view
- Proof of vision

**Vertical Slice (6-12 months):**
- Complete ship section
- One full day cycle (Lauds to Compline)
- Basic alien encounter
- Ship management basics
- Core gameplay loop

**Early Access (12-24 months):**
- Full ship explorable
- Multiple story arcs
- Alien species encounters
- Planetary visits
- Community feedback integration

**Full Release (24-36 months):**
- Complete narrative
- Multiple ships/communities
- VR support
- Mod support
- Christian community engagement
